About this project

Moving Forward is a student-designed prototype intended to support healthy coping and everyday routines after a difficult relationship ending or other emotional change.

It does not diagnose mental-health conditions or promise to make someone forget a person. It simply organizes gentle self-care tools in one place.

If someone feels unsafe, is thinking about hurting themselves, or cannot cope with everyday life, this website is not a substitute for professional help. They should tell a trusted adult, school counselor, parent/guardian, or qualified mental-health professional.
